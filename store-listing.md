## Full description (max 4000)
AudioScope turns your phone into a quick speaker/microphone test bench. It plays
test tones, listens with the microphone, and shows the loudest frequency it hears
back — so you can see whether a speaker reproduces the right frequency, and how
loud, across the whole audible range.

Three modes:

• Sweep — plays one frequency at a time from 20 Hz to 20 kHz and plots the
detected frequency against the one played. A faithful speaker sits on the flat
"perfect" line; departures show where it struggles.

• Multi-tone — plays many frequencies at once and checks each band in a single
pass, for a fast overall picture.

• Live — plays nothing and just shows a real-time spectrum of whatever the mic
hears: a voice, an instrument, a tone. Great for spotting the loudest pitch.

Features:
• Sub-bin-accurate frequency detection (FFT + interpolation).
• Volume gate slider to ignore background noise.
• The start button doubles as a live input-volume meter.
• Works fully offline — no account, no ads, no tracking.

Note: results reflect the whole speaker → air → microphone chain plus the room,
so it's a relative quality check, not a lab-calibrated instrument. Run it in a
quiet room with the media volume up for the cleanest results.
