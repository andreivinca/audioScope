# Privacy Policy — AudioScope

_Last updated: 6 June 2026_

AudioScope is a speaker/microphone frequency-response testing tool.

## What data we collect

**None.** AudioScope does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data.

## Microphone

AudioScope requests the **microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)** permission solely to
measure the sound coming back from your device's speaker while it plays test
tones.

- Audio from the microphone is analysed **in real time, entirely on your
  device**.
- Audio is **never recorded to a file, never stored, and never transmitted** off
  the device.
- No audio or any data derived from it leaves the app.

## Network

AudioScope does not use the internet. It contains no analytics, no advertising,
no third-party SDKs, and makes no network connections.

## Children

The app contains no data collection and is safe for all audiences.
